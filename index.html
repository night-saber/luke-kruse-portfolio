    About

    I'm Luke — an educator and builder from Oklahoma. I've spent years in classrooms and on the mat, and I bring the same approach to code: break the problem down, drill the fundamentals, then build something that actually works.

    Most of my work lives in the Minecraft ecosystem — Java plugins on Paper, Fabric mods, Skript systems, and custom resource packs with hand-built 3D models. Outside of that I build games in Godot and Unity, and web apps that run entirely on your own machine.

    Math is the through-line. Whether it's attack-speed curves, drop rates, or a voice-cloning pipeline, the interesting part is almost always the model underneath.
